Median is the middle delay (or mean of the two middle delays). P95 uses the nearest-rank value at ceiling(0.95 × count); with fewer than 20 observations it is the maximum. It is descriptive, not a stable performance guarantee.

Privacy, export and method limits

Labels, notes and timings stay in this page's memory. There are no uploads, analytics, external scripts, cookies or persistent storage. Refreshing or closing the page loses this log. Download it first if needed. The log holds at most 200 observations.

Intervals use the browser's monotonic clock. CSV quotes commas, quotes and line breaks. Text beginning with a spreadsheet formula marker, including after whitespace or control characters, gains a leading apostrophe for spreadsheet safety. JSON preserves the exact accepted text.

Trials interrupted by hiding the tab are discarded. Cancel does not create a result. Compare raw observations and ask intended readers about the captions; a short delay alone does not establish usability. No transcript is collected unless you choose to type one into a label or note.
